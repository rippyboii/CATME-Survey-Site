Before you begin

This survey asks you to evaluate every member of your team; including yourself; on ten aspects of team member effectiveness.

  • You will rate each member on a scale from 1 (strongly disagree) to 7 (strongly agree).
  • You can go back and change any rating before you submit.
  • At the end you will be prompted to download a PDF file that you need to turn in to the Canvas.
  • Please refer to the Teaching Team, if you need any assitance while completing the survey.
